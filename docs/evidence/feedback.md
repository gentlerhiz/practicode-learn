# Learner feedback

What learners tell us, and what we changed because of it. The first source is the pilot with PractiCode Academy learners, which hasn't started yet.

## How feedback is recorded

- **Findings, not opinions:** what learners did, where they got stuck, what they said, and how many of them.
- **What changed:** each finding links to the commit or lesson version that responded to it.
- **Quotes only with written consent,** with the attribution the learner chose (a first name, initials or anonymous). Consent records stay in the private archive.
- No names, emails or anything else that identifies a learner without that consent.
