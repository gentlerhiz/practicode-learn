Every lesson goes round the same loop

It's called PRIMM, and computing teachers across the UK swear by it. You read and guess before you write, so you're never stuck staring at a blank screen.

  1. 1Predict. Guess what the code will do.
  2. 2Run. See what really happens.
  3. 3Investigate. Pull it apart, one tap at a time.
  4. 4Modify. Change one thing and watch.
  5. 5Make. Build your own from scratch.
