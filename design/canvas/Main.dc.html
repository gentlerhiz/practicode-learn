Every lesson follows a loop proven in classrooms

It's called PRIMM, and computing teachers across the UK use it. You read and predict before you write, so you're never staring at a blank screen.

  1. 1

    Predict

    Guess what the code will do. Being wrong is useful.

  2. 2

    Run

    Run it and see. Were you right?

  3. 3

    Investigate

    Pull it apart with diagrams you control.

  4. 4

    Modify

    Change one thing. Watch what happens.

  5. 5

    Make

    Now build your own from scratch.

And every day, a four-minute review brings back the things you're about to forget.

Questions, answered

Still wondering about something? Email practicodeacademy@gmail.com and a real person will reply.

Is it really free to start?+

Yep. Module 1 of every track is free for good, and you don't need a card. If you want the rest, Pro starts with a 7-day free trial.

Why are there no videos?+

Honestly? People learn more by doing than by watching, and video eats data. You'll still see things move. The diagrams animate one step at a time, whenever you tap.

Can I learn on my phone?+

Yes. Every lesson works on a phone. For the bigger projects a laptop helps, and each track tells you upfront what you'll need.

Are the certificates recognised?+

They're verifiable digital badges that list exactly what you can do, mapped to frameworks like SFIA. They're not degrees, and we won't pretend they are. Your projects will do most of the talking.

How does offline learning work?+

Add PractiCode Learn to your home screen, download a module on Wi-Fi, and keep going without data. Your progress syncs next time you're online.
