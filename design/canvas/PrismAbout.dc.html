About PractiCode Learn

Job-ready skills, learned by doing.

PractiCode Learn is an interactive learning platform from PractiCode Academy in Nigeria. Learners build real skills in web development, data analysis, product design and AI by doing the work, with an AI tutor beside them and projects that check themselves.

Start Learning Free Work With Us

Our mission

Make job-ready digital skills practical and affordable for anyone willing to put in the practice.

Talent is spread evenly. Hands-on teaching at a fair price isn't. We're closing that gap, starting in Africa.

Our story

It started in a classroom.

PractiCode Academy teaches digital skills in three-month cohorts, in person in Ibadan and online, and runs a Women in Tech initiative alongside them.

One pattern kept showing up: progress came from practice, not from watching. Yet most online courses are built the other way round, as long videos that are hard to stream and easy to forget.

So we built PractiCode Learn around practice. Short lessons that ask you to act, feedback the moment you do, and a tutor for when you're stuck. It is the classroom we know works, without needing to be in the room.

How we teach

Built around practice. Measured against real standards.

Learn by doing

Every lesson step asks you to guess, try or build something, and shows you the result straight away.

Standards employers use

Each syllabus maps to a recognised framework: the MDN Curriculum, SFIA 9, Microsoft PL-300, ISO 9241-210 and ACM CS2023.

Real projects, checked

Every module ends with a project briefed like a real job, checked by automated tests and a rubric.

A tutor that coaches

The AI tutor knows the lesson you're on. It gives hints before answers and shows where each answer comes from.
