AI & Machine Learning · Module 7 · Testing a model

model.py

1model = LogisticRegression()
2model.fit(X_test, y_test)
3score = model.score(X_test, y_test)
4print(score) # 1.0

Why is my accuracy 100%? It feels too good to be true.

Good instinct. 100% is almost always a warning sign. Look at line 2. Which data is the model learning from, and which data are you testing it on?

Oh. The same data both times?

Exactly. It's like marking an exam the student has already seen. Train on X_train, y_train instead and run it again. Expect a lower number. That one you can trust.

Questions people ask us

Is it really free?+

Yep. Module 1 of every track is free for good, and you don't need a card. If you want the rest, Pro starts with a 7-day free trial.

Why no videos?+

Honestly? People learn more by doing than by watching, and video eats data. Things still move. The diagrams animate one step at a time, whenever you tap.

Can I really learn on my phone?+

Most of it, yes. Lessons, reviews and the AI tutor work in your phone's browser. Some modules need a computer: Power BI work needs a Windows PC, and Figma design work needs a laptop. Each track page lists what you'll need.

Will employers care about the certificate?+

It's a verifiable badge that lists exactly what you can do, mapped to frameworks like SFIA. It isn't a degree, and we won't pretend it is. Your projects will do most of the talking.

Does the AI tutor just give me the answers?+

No, and that's on purpose. It explains the idea, points you to the line or step that matters, and gives hints first. If you've had a proper go and you're still stuck, it will walk you through the answer. Free accounts get 5 questions a day and Pro gets 50.
