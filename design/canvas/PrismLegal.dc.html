Privacy, terms and accessibility

Plain language first. Last updated 3 October 2026.Draft for legal review

{{docTitle}}

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to teach you and run your account.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You can download or delete your data at any time from Settings.
  • Questions you ask the AI tutor are never used to train AI models.

What we collect

Your name, email or phone number, and country when you sign up. Your progress, answers and projects while you learn. Payment records if you subscribe (your card details stay with our payment provider). Age range, gender and employment status only if you choose to share them, for anonymous impact reports.

Why we use it

To teach you: tracking progress, scheduling your daily review and checking projects. To run your account and payments. To improve lessons, using anonymous, combined data. Analytics only run if you agree to them.

The AI tutor

When you ask the tutor a question, we send it, the lesson you are on and your code to our AI provider. We keep these conversations for 30 days to check quality and safety, then delete them. You can clear your history in Settings.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download or delete your data, and object to how we use it. Most of this is self-service in Settings. For anything else, email us and we will reply within the time the law requires. We follow Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 and the GDPR.

The short version

  • Be kind in the community, and do your own work on assessments.
  • Lessons and the PractiCode name belong to us. Your projects belong to you.
  • Cancel any time in one click. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Certificates show the skills you demonstrated. They are not a degree.

Your account

You need to be at least 13 to use PractiCode Learn. Keep your login to yourself. You are responsible for what happens on your account.

Paying and cancelling

Prices are shown in your currency before you pay. Subscriptions renew until you cancel, and we remind you before a yearly renewal. If something goes wrong with a payment, email us and a person will help.

Our goal

Every part of PractiCode Learn should work for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, zoom or reduced motion. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA.

What we do

  • Every animation has a still version, and turns off when you choose reduced motion.
  • Colour is never the only way we show something. Icons and words come with it.
  • Lessons work with the keyboard, and are tested with screen readers.
  • Text meets contrast guidelines on our dark backgrounds.

Tell us what isn't working

If something gets in your way, email practicodeacademy@gmail.com. We reply within 5 working days and tell you when it is fixed.
